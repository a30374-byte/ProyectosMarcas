Top 10 Memes

Here is the list of the best 10 Memes based on a professional Memenaliser's criteria.

Top 10: JoJo's Secret Paper Gun

A meme originated from Japanese anime Diamond
is Unbreakable, the fourth part of Japanese
famous anime JoJo's Bizarre Adventure, episode
33, the manga chapter was printed on April 1995.

The meme shows a scene of the series in which
protagonist Josuke inspects Tomuko's pockets and
finds a folded piece of paper. Upon unfolding it,
the paper reveals to be blank. However, when he
least expects it, a gun appears from behind the
paper and shoots Josuke.
The meme became popular with the raise of JoJo's
popularity around 2015.

Top 9: His Power is Over 9000

A meme that became famous on 2006, it comes
from an English dub of the japanese series
'The Return of Goku'.

In the scene, Vegetta's 'scouter', a device
which reads the ki of beings and represents
them as a number, displays Goku's ki at a
number over 9000.
In rage, Vegetta shatters his 'scouter'.

Top 8: Old Man Yells at Cloud

A meme that comes from a sight gag introduced
in famous series The Simpson's Episode 13,
Season 13, "The Old Man and the Key."


The meme became famous on the internet on
November 16th 2009 when Halolz user Shawn
Handyside submitted a photoshopped version
of the scene, in which the cloud is replaced
by Final Fantasy VII protagonist Cloud
Strife.
DeviantArt user Badonk submitted a similar meme
the same day.

Top 7: His Greed Was His Downfall

A meme derived from X's viral meme 'His Greed
Sickens Me', a reply to a post with a screenshot
of TikTok user goodnight.mewostar's viral video,
in which he showed his cat holding an apple.

The meme became famous on the internet on
January 30 2024 when X user Skipy_arts posted
a version of the 'His Greed Sickens Me' meme,
showing two pictures. One with the original cat
holding the apple, and another of a cat with 2
two apples on its head, accompanied by a quite
dramatic caption
Remember not to be greedy, dear reader.

Top 6: Omae Wa Mou Shindeirou

A meme originated from Japanese manga and anime
series Fist of the North Star's scene in which
the protagonist Kenshiro says the catchphrase
'Omae Wa Mou Shindeirou', which translates as
'You Are Already Dead'.

The meme was already popular by June 11th 2011,
when NeoGAF Forums user Nekofrog created a thread
asking other members what memes they were getting
tired of, captioning 'Omae Wa Mou Shindeirou' as
a meme that was often used incorrectly.
The meme exploded in popularity due to remixes in YT
made in the summer of 2017.

Top 5: Who Killed Hannibal?

A meme originated from the show The Eric Andre Show,
in the skit host Eric Andre shoots co-host Hannibal
Buress multiple times in the chest. Then, he turns
around and asks 'Who Killed Hannibal?


The meme became popular on October 16th when Facebook
meme page 'Holy shit dude is that a ᵐᵉᵐᵉ' uploaded the
clip. Screenshots of the clip allowed it to become an
exploitable object labeling meme in April of 2018,
with the phrasal sentence 'Why Would X Do This?
A version of the meme gained 32000 points on Reddit.

Top 4: Change My Mind

A meme that appeared when Far-Right conservative
commentator, Steven Crowder posted a picture of
himself in February 2018. The photo was taken
during his recurrent activity, he used to go to
colleges and debated with students about different
extreme topics.


The meme became popular due to him crossing the line,
and Reddit and X users immediately parodied the image.
The first meme of the photo is often atributed to X
user IanWhetstone who modified the photo, in its parody
the paper sign has written 'The Clitoris is a Myth'.
Oftentimes your extreme ideologies will make you become
an internet meme.

Top 2: Pepe Frog

Pepe the Frog is a comic character and Internet
meme created by cartoonist Matt Furie. Pepe
originated in 2005 webcomic Boy's Club, and went
on to become a popular meme and internet character


The character gained popularity from 2008 onwards on
pages like 4chan and MySpace; by 2015, it had become
one of the most popular memes in 4chan and MySpace.
There are many variations of the Pepe Frog meme in
the internet.

Top 1: Rickrolling

A meme that doubles down as a prank too, it
consists in sending an unexpected video of
English singer Rick Astley's hit song 'Never
Gonna Give You Up'

Rickrolling originated in 15 May 2007 when
4Chan user Shawn Cotter uploaded the 'Never
Gonna Give You Up' full music video to YT
and linked to it instead of a trailer of
Grand Theft Auto IV.
In some way, we too have Rickrolled you.
